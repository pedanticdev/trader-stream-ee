# Expected output for a 60-second baseline recording at roughly 60K msg/sec

## C4 baseline (typical)

Longest pause:        0.2 - 1.0 ms (often shown as "0 ms" by JMC due to rounding)
Collector name:       GPGC New
GC cause:             Concurrent New collection
Pauses over 5 ms:     0
Total pause time:     < 10 ms across the recording

## G1 baseline (typical)

Longest pause:        5 - 15 ms (depends on host load)
Collector name:       G1 Young Generation
GC cause:             G1 Evacuation Pause
Pauses over 5 ms:     2 - 6
Total pause time:     30 - 80 ms across the recording

## Interpretation

Even at steady state and with a comfortable heap, G1 incurs single-digit
millisecond young-gen pauses periodically. C4 does not, because all phases
including young-gen collection are concurrent with the application.

For a 60K msg/sec workload, a 10 ms pause means roughly 600 messages buffered.
Whether that is acceptable depends on your SLA - this is the kind of question
Module 5 returns to.
